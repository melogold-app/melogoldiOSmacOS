- Now Playing: the background is the blurred cover, alive with the music — it flows, blobs of its colors merge and part on the bass, and it pulses on the beat. It freezes while paused; turn motion off with Live background in Settings. The player screen is always dark with light text and controls.
- Now Playing in a wide window: the cover and the lyrics form one block in the middle, and the lyric size follows the window height.
- While the big cover loads, the small one shows instead of a music note.
- A song no longer stops after the first megabyte: after YouTube refuses, a fresh URL comes from a new session, up to four times with pauses.
- If the stream breaks mid-song (network or VPN server change), the song continues from the same second instead of restarting.
- When the network drops, the track waits for it in place instead of being skipped.
- Handing playback to another device continues the song from the same second, and so does Listen Here.
- Albums play the album's songs instead of music videos (Tyler, The Creator's LEMONHEAD is 2:11, not the 1:25 video). Videos already saved in Liked and playlists are replaced by the songs once, keeping the date and position.
- The catalog is no longer cut down to Russia: artist pages show the new albums.
- Find Other Lyrics: when LrcLib doesn't respond, it says so, with a Retry button.
- Mac: the "All · Music · YouTube" bar from Search no longer shows through Now Playing.
