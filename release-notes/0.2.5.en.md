- Mac: updates download on their own. When a new version is ready, "Version … is ready · Relaunch" appears above the player — one click and the app relaunches already updated. It checks once an hour. This works starting with the update after this version.
- Mac: the artist page looks like Music: a full-width photo up to the top of the window, a large name, About the Artist, Play and Subscribe buttons; the name moves into the title once the header scrolls away. Trackpad scrolling no longer sticks or stutters.
- Mac: the track title and player buttons are white (black in the light theme) instead of grey, like Music on macOS 26. The same goes for the icons on the right of the capsule and the lyrics.
- Lyrics grow with the window: lines are larger in a wide window (up to 64 pt) and the same as before in a narrow one.
- Covers of older music videos (such as "Shape of You") are no longer replaced by a grey picture with three dots — in the app, on the lock screen and in the tags of saved files.
- Apple Watch: after you tap a track, the watch asks "Where to Listen?" — account devices on top, "On This Watch" (AirPods and other headphones) below.
